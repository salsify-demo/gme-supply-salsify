Why it matters now

A distributor's catalog is only as good as its slowest supplier

GME Supply does not control when DBI Sala changes a part number or when Milwaukee ships a new image set. The content arrives in hundreds of formats, and the team has to normalize, enrich and publish it before a customer searches for it. Salsify was built for exactly this intake-and-enrich loop.

Supplier onboarding

Hundreds of brands send spreadsheets, PDFs and portal exports. Each one needs mapping into GME's attribute standard.

Enrichment at scale

Fall protection buyers filter on capacity, standards (ANSI Z359, OSHA), connector type and length. Missing attributes mean missing search results.

Ecommerce plus field sales

The same record feeds the website, the quote tool and the printed catalog, so it has to be right once.

The pattern we see

Where product content usually breaks down

We have not audited GME Supply's systems. What follows is the pattern we see at specialty industrial distributors and the questions we would like to check against yours.

Supplier content arrives raw

  • Spreadsheets, PDFs and portal exports from hundreds of brands, each in its own structure
  • Product data is re-keyed into the ecommerce platform and the ERP separately
  • Images and spec sheets are chased by email when a listing is missing them

Enrichment is manual

  • Standards, capacity and compatibility attributes are filled in by hand per SKU
  • New products wait in a queue before they are searchable on the site
  • Nobody knows which listings are incomplete until a customer calls

What a product change looks like with Salsify

1. Change onceA product manager updates a spec, image or description in Salsify, or it flows in from a supplier feed.
2. WorkflowThe right approvers sign off in their queue. Rules apply automatically.
3. ValidateReadiness reports check the record against every destination's current spec and image rules.
4. PublishOne click sends the correct format to gmesupply.com, the ERP and the print catalog.
5. MeasurePage grades and compliance scores come back into Salsify so the team sees what landed.
